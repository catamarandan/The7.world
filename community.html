Community & Legacy

Community & Legacy at THE 7

An ambition to support local participation, youth development and host-community opportunity.

THE 7 / 01

Youth engagement

Potential clinics, mentoring, school exchanges and opportunities to meet athletes and creators.

THE 7 / 02

Local economic participation

Seek meaningful access for local vendors, craftspeople, cultural organizations and service providers.

THE 7 / 03

Responsible operations

Assess waste, transport, marine protection, accessibility and community impacts before an event is approved.

THE 7 / 04

Measure what matters

Any future claims about attendance, economic activity or community outcomes should rely on verified reporting.

A lasting local benefit

A proposed legacy program should be developed with local stakeholders and measured through real outcomes, not generic promises.

Youth opportunities

Coaching clinics, introductions to new sports and mentoring with qualified supervision.

Local enterprise

Accessible opportunities for Bahamian vendors, artists, guides and suppliers.

Skills and employment

Event production, hospitality and media training opportunities where feasible.

Environmental responsibility

Waste reduction, marine protection and practical coastal stewardship.

Be part of the beginning.

Conversations are welcome with prospective hosts, cultural leaders, sporting organizations, media partners and businesses interested in developing this vision responsibly.
