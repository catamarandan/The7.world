The Vision

The Vision at THE 7

A global platform where athletic excellence and cultural identity share the spotlight.

THE 7 / 01

Sport and culture as equals

The competition is intended to be credible and organized; the cultural program is intended to be a headline attraction, not background entertainment.

THE 7 / 02

An international identity

THE 7 is designed as a recognizable festival concept that could eventually travel to other destinations while reflecting the character of each host.

THE 7 / 03

Shared value

Our guiding ambition is a festival that creates meaningful opportunities for local businesses, performers, athletes, communities, visitors and responsible partners.

An invitation to build responsibly

THE 7 is intended as a repeatable, internationally relevant gathering whose inaugural edition is still being explored. Its scale, timing and location depend on partners, permissions and feasibility.
