The Games

The Games at THE 7

A proposed sporting program spanning field, pitch, beach and water.

THE 7 / 01

American football

Exploratory high-school and college football showcases could become signature events. Teams, eligibility, travel, sanctioning, broadcast rights and venues remain to be established.

THE 7 / 02

Rugby Sevens

A fast-moving international rugby format aligned with the seven-day identity; tournament structure and governing-body participation remain exploratory.

THE 7 / 03

Cricket

Cricket offers a strong regional connection and opportunities for exhibition or tournament formats developed with appropriate organizers.

THE 7 / 04

Bahamian sloop sailing

Traditional Bahamian sloop racing could celebrate local seamanship and maritime heritage with experienced local organizers.

THE 7 / 05

Beach and community sports

Beach volleyball, beach soccer, basketball, paddle sports, swimming and youth clinics are candidate activities, not announced competitions.

THE 7 / 06

Competition integrity

Every confirmed event would require qualified officials, rules, athlete welfare, safeguarding, medical response, insurance and appropriate permissions.

A proposed multi-venue sports program

Explore football and rugby at suitable licensed fields; cricket at an approved pitch; Bahamian sloop sailing in suitable local waters; and beach sports only where permits, access and environmental safeguards allow. These are programming concepts, not booked venues.

Football

High-school showcase and college finale, subject to eligibility, school approval and governing rules.

Rugby Sevens

Tournament format and team invitations to be developed with rugby organizers.

Cricket

Regional competition or exhibition format, subject to facility and scheduling review.

Sailing

Traditional Bahamian sloop racing in partnership with local sailors and maritime authorities.

Beach & water

Beach volleyball, beach soccer, paddle activities and supervised family recreation.
