THE 7 / 01

A new kind of gathering

Imagine American football under the lights, Rugby Sevens at full speed, cricket on the green, traditional Bahamian sloops offshore, and beaches alive with sport. Then imagine Junkanoo, music, food, art and shared celebration at the heart of the experience.

THE 7 / 02

A destination with a story

The Bahamas is being explored as a potential inaugural host. Nassau offers a compelling cultural and maritime setting, but no host agreement or venue allocation is in place.

THE 7 / 03

Help shape the beginning

THE 7 is an invitation to prospective sporting bodies, host representatives, cultural organizations, hospitality businesses, media companies and supporters to explore a shared opportunity.

What makes THE 7 different

THE 7 is designed around a single destination experience in which sport, culture, hospitality and storytelling reinforce each other. It is currently a proposal, with Nassau being explored as a potential inaugural setting.

Competition

Multiple sports across fields, beaches and water.

Culture

Junkanoo, music, cuisine and Bahamian creative leadership.

Destination

An immersive visitor experience rather than a single stadium event.

Connection

A framework for meaningful partnerships and community participation.

Be part of the beginning.

Conversations are welcome with prospective hosts, cultural leaders, sporting organizations, media partners and businesses interested in developing this vision responsibly.
