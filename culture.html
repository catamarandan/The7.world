The Culture

The Culture at THE 7

Music, dance, cuisine, crafts, storytelling and Bahamian traditions envisioned as major attractions.

THE 7 / 01

Junkanoo and heritage

A Bahamian-led cultural program could highlight Junkanoo traditions through authentic participation and collaboration with cultural practitioners.

THE 7 / 02

Live music and performance

Concerts, local performers, guest artists and evening entertainment are proposed, with no performers yet contracted.

THE 7 / 03

Food and hospitality

Local chefs, seafood, traditional dishes, restaurant partners and small vendors could introduce visitors to Bahamian flavors.

THE 7 / 04

Arts and exchange

Markets, artists, storytellers, workshops and cultural exchange between participating communities could enrich the entire week.

Bahamian culture at the center

The cultural program should be designed with Bahamian performers, makers, chefs and community groups—not simply imported entertainment.

Junkanoo

Traditional costumes, music and parade heritage led by local cultural participants.

Food & hospitality

Conch dishes, local seafood, restaurants and food entrepreneurs.

Music & dance

Bahamian performers alongside international guests, subject to artist agreements.

Crafts & heritage

Artisan demonstrations, storytelling and opportunities for locally owned businesses.

Be part of the beginning.

Conversations are welcome with prospective hosts, cultural leaders, sporting organizations, media partners and businesses interested in developing this vision responsibly.
